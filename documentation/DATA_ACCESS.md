# Data access and publication scope

This public package contains author-produced final aggregate analysis tables and the code needed to reproduce calculations from those tables.

Included: country-period and country-month aggregates; exposure/cohort group rates; cluster-level regression cross-products; specification definitions; final figure values; reference results; hashes and reproduction instructions.

Not distributed: original downloaded archives or database extracts; scraped pages or legal/source passages; medicine-level identifiers, matches or status histories; individual medicine weights; detailed source/linkage ledgers; credentials or correspondence drafts.

The public package starts after source extraction, medicine matching and individual weight construction. It is numerical replication from final analysis tables, not independent reconstruction of the original collected corpus. Source-publisher ownership and access conditions are unchanged. Requests concerning additional research materials should be directed to the corresponding author identified in the SSRN paper, subject to applicable access conditions.

The aggregate tables incorporate the chosen medicine and country weights. Their upstream estimation is outside the public final-table replication stage.
